Welcome To Judy's

Taste the Bold and Spicy Sichuan Flavours

Judy's Sichuan Cuisine brings authentic Szechuan cooking to Virginia Beach - bold chili heat, fragrant Sichuan pepper, and the comforting dishes that make Sichuan cuisine legendary.

From Steak in Hot Chili Oil to "Mapo" Tofu and handmade "Xiao-long-bao", every plate is crafted with freshness, quality, and the seven classic flavors of Sichuan cooking.
